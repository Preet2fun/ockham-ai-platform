# Hearth — Ideal Customer Profile

Pre-launch: every segment fact below is a hypothesis to validate, not a measured
finding. Market sizing is `[Assumed]` until `../../data-analysis/` holds real
numbers.

## Firmographic

- **US restaurant groups with 2–20 locations** under one ownership.
- **Delivery-heavy formats:** fast-casual, QSR, pizza, and casual concepts
  where delivery and takeout are a large share of orders. This fits Hearth's
  data model (orders → deliveries → payments).
- **No in-house analyst or BI team.** This is the single most important
  qualifier. Reporting today is POS exports, spreadsheets and gut feel.

## Organizational

- **Economic buyer: the owner** or owner-group partner. Buys on margin,
  control and time saved.
- **Daily user: the operations director or area/general manager.** Runs
  several locations, is on the floor or at a counter tablet between tasks,
  and is not technical.
- One small leadership team owns every location's operations. Nobody owns
  "analysis" as a job.

## Technographic

- Already runs a POS and one or more delivery channels: own ordering plus
  third-party marketplaces.
- Data is spread across those tools, with no single place that joins orders,
  menu, delivery and payment outcomes.
- Comfortable with SaaS on a tablet or laptop. Has no data warehouse.

## Behavioural triggers

- **Opening a third (or next) location.** What worked by instinct at one site
  stops scaling.
- **Margin squeeze:** rising food, labour or marketplace-commission costs,
  with no clear view of which items, dayparts or channels lose money.
- **Delivery complaints or bad reviews** about lateness, with no way to see
  where the time goes.
- **Payment leakage:** failed or disputed payments discovered late during
  reconciliation.

## Disqualifiers

- **Single-location independents.** Too little cross-location pain and data,
  and highly price-sensitive.
- **Chains with 50+ locations** or groups with a BI or analytics team. They
  already have tooling and analysts, and buy enterprise suites through formal
  evaluations.
- **Full-service dine-in only.** Their key data (covers, table turns,
  reservations) is outside Hearth's model.
- **Outside the US**, for now. Different marketplace and payment landscapes,
  plus data-protection obligations (e.g. GDPR) not yet designed for.
- **Wants the AI to run the business unattended.** Hearth's AI explains and
  proposes. Changes always go through an approval card.
