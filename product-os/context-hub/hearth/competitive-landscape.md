# Hearth — Competitive Landscape

## The fixed set: use these 6 for all Hearth research and references

**Verification status:** the "what to study" notes are starting hypotheses
from general market knowledge. **None has been verified against vendor
sources yet.** Discovery stage 03 and PRD stage 04 verify each one before it
is cited as fact, and record the source and date here.

| # | Competitor | Type | Study for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Toast** | POS and restaurant platform | Built-in reporting and its AI assistant features: how far the incumbent POS goes toward "explain my business," and whether it covers multi-location groups |
| 2 | **Square for Restaurants** | POS and payments | Reporting depth for small multi-location operators, and any AI assistant features |
| 3 | **Restaurant365** | Back-office suite (accounting, inventory, labour, reporting) | Multi-unit back-office reporting: the "all-in-one back office" alternative and its analytics and AI story |
| 4 | **Olo** | Digital ordering and delivery platform | Delivery and channel performance data, and what operators can see about delivery timing and channel profitability |
| 5 | **Tenzo** | Restaurant analytics and forecasting | The closest "analytics for restaurants" comparator: insight and forecasting claims, and who it serves |
| 6 | **Status quo: POS exports + spreadsheets** | Workaround | What the operator actually does today, and the hours and lateness involved. This is the real competitor for a group with no analyst |

## Where Hearth must win

- **Explanation over reporting.** "Why" plus a next step, not another
  dashboard.
- **Joined data.** Orders, menu, delivery and payment outcomes are reasoned
  over together, where incumbents typically split them across products.
- **Grounded, tenant-only answers** that show their evidence.
